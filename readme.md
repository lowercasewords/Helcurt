# Helcurt

## Unique traits of Helcurt

- Assasin
- Poke combatant
- Strikes from the shadows

## Integration thought process

- Helcurt of MLBB: Eliminate enemy -> Increase of stats through equipment -> Back to step 1
- Helcurt of SRB2 should instead have the elimination grant reward the player with something unique to SRB2 gameplay loop.

## Integration of Abilities From MLBB to SRB2

### Shadow of Styx

Primary Focus (game revolves around this)

- Stealth mode (Enabler)
  - Helcurt is in the most safety here
  - Indefinite time until exposed
  - Goal is to find eliminate enemies

- Ambush mode (Payoff)
  - Helcurt is the most dangerous here
  - 1 or 2 seconds max duration, or duration experimentally could be extended by Deadly Stinger Ability
  - End goal

- Exposed mode (Retreat)
  - Helcurt is the most vulnerable here
  - Indefinite until concealed
  - Goal is to retreat into the shadow

### Hidden Terror

- Stealth mode only ability
  - Supporting ability to 'Shadow of Styx'

### Deadly Stinger

- Exposed mode only ability
  - Supporting ability to 'Shadow of Styx'

- Few second automatic damage window
  - Visually several slashes of stingers and scythes

  - AOE pattern
  - Momentum is slowed slightly
